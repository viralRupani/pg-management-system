Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Basera Technologies ("Basera", "we", "us") operates the Basera PG management platform — a web dashboard for managers and a resident mobile app. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information we collect

From PG managers and owners

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, and the name of your PG or company.
  • PG configuration: building names, room layouts, bed details, rent amounts, and other property settings you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited, actions taken in the dashboard, and session metadata (browser type, device, IP address).

From residents

  • Identity data: name, phone number, and (optionally) email address — entered by the manager at registration.
  • KYC documents: photos of government-issued ID that you upload for verification. Stored encrypted on AWS S3.
  • Payment screenshots: images of UPI payment confirmations you upload. Stored encrypted on AWS S3.
  • Rent and deposit records: amounts, dates, and status of invoices and transactions.

Automatically collected

  • Server logs including request timestamps, IP addresses, and HTTP status codes, retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes.
  • Push notification tokens (Expo/FCM) if you enable notifications in the resident app.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the platform — managing beds, rent, KYC, deposits, and resident communications within your PG.
  • To send OTP codes for resident login and password-reset emails for managers.
  • To send in-app and email notifications (rent reminders, payment confirmations, announcements).
  • To bill you — we count active (bed-allocated) residents at month-end to calculate your ₹5-per-resident fee.
  • To investigate security incidents and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal data to third parties. We do not use resident data for advertising.

3. Data storage and security

  • Your data is stored on AWS infrastructure (database servers and S3 file storage), primarily in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
  • Each PG's data is strictly isolated using Postgres row-level security. No other PG owner or manager can access your residents, payments, or documents.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel only.

4. Data retention

We retain your account and PG data for as long as your account is active. Residents and their records are retained after move-out (required for deposit settlement history and KYC compliance). You may request deletion of your account and associated data — see Section 6.

5. Sharing with third parties

We use the following service providers who may process your data:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): hosting, database (RDS), file storage (S3), and transactional email (SES).
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): push notification delivery to the resident app.
  • Cloudflare: CDN and DDoS protection for the marketing site.

Each provider processes data only as directed by us and under data processing agreements.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data — managers can update most information directly in the dashboard.
  • Delete your account and data. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Object to processing for purposes other than service delivery.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at viralrupani12017@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Request".

7. Cookies

The Basera dashboard uses session cookies strictly necessary for authentication. The marketing site (baserapg.com) does not set any tracking or analytics cookies. No third-party tracking pixels or ad-retargeting scripts are loaded.

8. Children's privacy

Basera is a business tool intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified by email to account holders at least 7 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
