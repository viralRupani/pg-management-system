1. The service

Basera provides a software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform for managing paying-guest (PG) accommodations — including resident management, rent invoicing, KYC document storage, deposit tracking, complaints, and a resident mobile app.

The platform is intended for use by PG owners and managers in India. Short-stay and long-term resident users access the platform through credentials issued by their PG manager.

2. Free trial

New accounts receive a 30-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial period, billing begins at the rates described in Section 3.

3. Billing

Price: ₹5 (Indian Rupees) per active resident per month. An "active resident" is a resident who has been allocated a bed at any point during the billing month. Residents who have not been allocated a bed (e.g. upcoming bookings, short-stay guests, or moved-out residents) are not counted.

Billing cycle: Invoices are generated at the end of each calendar month based on a snapshot of your active-resident headcount.

Payment: Payments are accepted via bank transfer or UPI. Invoices are sent by email to the account owner. If an invoice is unpaid for more than 30 days, access to the platform may be suspended until the outstanding balance is settled.

Price changes: We will give at least 30 days' advance notice by email before changing our pricing.
